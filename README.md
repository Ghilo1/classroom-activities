# Classroom activities: estimate before you calculate

Free, printable math activities for middle school (about grades 6 to 8), written by a former middle school math teacher.

They all use one routine:

1. **Estimate** the answer out loud and write it on the board.
2. **Calculate** step by step, with the units on every line.
3. **Ask:** *does this make sense?*

Students who struggle gain the most from it: with an estimate to compare against, they can find on their own the line where a calculation went wrong, instead of just getting a red cross.

## Activities

- [**Does this make sense? 10 estimate-first problems**](estimate-first.md): speed, area, volume, percentages and unit conversions, each with an estimate, the most common wrong answer and a step-by-step solution.

Some problems link to a free calculator on [Calcupia](https://calcupia.com/) that shows the formula and a worked example, so students can check their own work.
