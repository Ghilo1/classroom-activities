# Does this make sense? 10 estimate-first problems

Each problem follows the same routine:

1. **Estimate** the answer out loud before anyone touches a calculator. Write the guess on the board.
2. **Calculate** step by step, with the units written on every line.
3. **Compare** the result with the estimate and ask: *does this make sense?*

Every problem also lists the wrong answer students give most often, so you can ask the class whether it makes sense before revealing the correct one.

Level: about grades 6 to 8 (ages 11 to 14). Metric units.

---

## 1. The cyclist

A cyclist rides 4.5 km in 15 minutes. What is the cyclist's speed in km/h?

- **Estimate:** a bike usually goes 15 to 20 km/h.
- **Common wrong answer:** 4.5 ÷ 15 = **0.3 km/h**. *Could you walk faster than that?*
- **Solution:**
  - 15 min = 15 ÷ 60 h = 0.25 h
  - speed = 4.5 km ÷ 0.25 h = **18 km/h**, inside the estimate.

Check it with the [speed, distance and time calculator](https://calcupia.com/science/speed-distance-time/).

## 2. The classroom floor

A classroom is 8 m long and 6.5 m wide. What is its area?

- **Estimate:** a classroom is roughly 50 m².
- **Common wrong answer:** measuring in centimeters, 800 × 650 = 520,000, then writing **520,000 m²**. *Is our classroom bigger than ten soccer fields?*
- **Solution:** 8 m × 6.5 m = **52 m²**.

## 3. Walking to school

The school is 1.2 km away and you walk at 5 km/h. How long does the walk take?

- **Estimate:** a little over a kilometer on foot is about a quarter of an hour.
- **Common wrong answer:** 1.2 ÷ 5 = 0.24, then writing **0.24 min** or **24 min**. *0.24 of an hour is not 24 minutes.*
- **Solution:**
  - time = 1.2 km ÷ 5 km/h = 0.24 h
  - 0.24 h × 60 min/h = **14.4 min**, about 14 minutes.

## 4. Glasses of water

How many 250 mL glasses can you fill from a 1.5 L bottle?

- **Estimate:** a big bottle fills a handful of glasses.
- **Common wrong answer:** 1.5 ÷ 250 = **0.006 glasses**. *Not even one glass from a full bottle?*
- **Solution:**
  - 1.5 L = 1,500 mL
  - 1,500 mL ÷ 250 mL = **6 glasses**.

Convert units with the [volume converter](https://calcupia.com/converters/volume-converter/).

## 5. Fuel for a trip

A car uses 6 L of fuel per 100 km. How much fuel does a 250 km trip need?

- **Estimate:** 250 km is a bit more than two "hundreds", so a bit more than 12 L.
- **Common wrong answer:** 250 × 6 = **1,500 L**. *A car's fuel tank usually holds 40 to 70 L.*
- **Solution:**
  - 250 km ÷ 100 km = 2.5
  - 2.5 × 6 L = **15 L**.

See the [fuel cost calculator](https://calcupia.com/automotive/fuel-cost-calculator/).

## 6. The sale

A $40 T-shirt is 25% off. What is the sale price?

- **Estimate:** a quarter off $40 is about $10 off.
- **Common wrong answer:** 40 − 25 = **$15**. *Did the store take off more than half?*
- **Solution:**
  - discount = 25% of $40 = 0.25 × $40 = $10
  - sale price = $40 − $10 = **$30**.

Try it with the [discount calculator](https://calcupia.com/daily/discount-calculator/).

## 7. The concrete slab

A patio slab is 5 m long, 4 m wide and 15 cm thick. How much concrete does it need, in m³?

- **Estimate:** 20 m² of floor, a few centimeters thick: a few cubic meters.
- **Common wrong answer:** 5 × 4 × 15 = **300 m³**. *That is a hundred times our estimate.*
- **Solution:**
  - 15 cm = 0.15 m
  - 5 m × 4 m × 0.15 m = **3 m³**.

See the [concrete volume calculator](https://calcupia.com/construction/concrete-calculator/).

## 8. The 10 km run

A runner finishes 10 km in 50 minutes. What is the average speed in km/h?

- **Estimate:** a runner is faster than a walker (5 km/h) and slower than a cyclist (about 20 km/h).
- **Common wrong answer:** 10 ÷ 50 = **0.2 km/h**. *Would a runner be slower than someone walking?*
- **Solution:**
  - 50 min = 50 ÷ 60 h ≈ 0.833 h
  - 10 km ÷ 0.833 h = **12 km/h**, which is a pace of 5 minutes per kilometer.

## 9. A long drive

A car drives at 60 km/h for 3 hours 45 minutes. How far does it go?

- **Estimate:** almost 4 hours at 60 km/h: a bit under 240 km.
- **Common wrong answer:** writing 3 h 45 min as 3.45 h, then 3.45 × 60 = **207 km**. *Is 45 minutes really 0.45 of an hour?*
- **Solution:**
  - 45 min = 45 ÷ 60 h = 0.75 h, so 3 h 45 min = 3.75 h
  - 3.75 h × 60 km/h = **225 km**.

Convert durations with the [time converter](https://calcupia.com/converters/time-converter/).

## 10. The pencil

A pencil is 0.18 m long. How long is it in centimeters?

- **Estimate:** a pencil is shorter than a ruler (30 cm) and longer than a finger.
- **Common wrong answer:** 0.18 ÷ 100 = **0.0018 cm**. *Thinner than a sheet of paper?*
- **Solution:** 1 m = 100 cm, so 0.18 m × 100 = **18 cm**.

Check with the [length converter](https://calcupia.com/converters/length-converter/).

---

## Tips for the teacher

- The estimate does not need to be accurate. It only needs to exist, so the final answer has something to be compared against.
- When an answer is absurd, do not mark it wrong right away. Ask a real-world question ("Could you walk faster than that?") and let the student find the line where it went wrong.
- After a few weeks, students start asking "Does this make sense?" before you do.
